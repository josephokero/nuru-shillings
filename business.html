Business

Take payment inside your own platform · Updated 10 October 2026

The short version
  • Any shop, app, marketplace, or site can accept Nuru Shillings from any wallet, in any country.
  • Checkout shows your company name and the amount. The buyer’s name is not put on display.
  • You pay 1% of each payment that is paid. No setup fee, no monthly fee, and no fee on refusals.
  • Your earnings land in your Nuru balance and can be withdrawn to PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa for free.
  • Start in Sandbox today. Verify once to take live payments.

Developers can go straight to the Developers overview or the full Docs.

01Who it is for

Nuru is for any business that already sells something online and wants another way to get paid, one that works across borders and keeps the buyer private.

  • Shops selling physical or digital goods;
  • Apps and SaaS selling plans, credits, or features;
  • Marketplaces that collect for many sellers;
  • Creators and communities selling access or content;
  • Agencies and freelancers sending invoices.

02What your customers see

You create a payment, and the buyer is sent to a Nuru checkout that shows your company name and the amount. They confirm from their wallet. If they do not have a wallet yet, they can create one on the spot with their email and return to the same checkout.

  • The buyer pays exactly the amount you set. Nuru adds no fee on their side.
  • Buyers without a balance can add money first with PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa.
  • The payment is settled on Nuru’s private chain. It is not written to a public ledger.

03How you get paid

When a payment is paid, the amount less 1% is added to your Nuru balance at once. Your server receives payment.paid on its webhook, and the payment appears in Business tools with its reference.

You can spend that balance, send it, or withdraw it at 100 NSH to 1 USD to PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa in your verified name. Withdrawing is free and is sent after payout review.

05Business tools

Business tools is the dashboard for taking payments. From it you can:

  • create and replace Sandbox and Live keys;
  • save a webhook URL and send a test event;
  • create a payment by hand and open its checkout link, without writing code;
  • see every payment, its status, its fee, and your reference;
  • refund a paid payment in one click;
  • see every webhook event Nuru sent and the reply your server gave.

07Accepted businesses

Card processors often refuse whole categories, including adult content. Nuru does not refuse a lawful business for its category. A shop, an app, a marketplace, or an adult site can take payment.

Nuru does not accept businesses that are illegal where they or their buyers are, including fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion, weapons, illegal drugs, stolen data, material that exploits children, unlicensed gambling, and pyramid or “guaranteed return” schemes. The full list is in Acceptable use.

08Refunds and complaints

You set your own refund policy and show it on your site. A refund always returns the full amount to the buyer at the same rate, and returns the 1% fee to you. Your balance must cover the amount you received.

You are responsible for what you sell, for delivery, and for your own customer service. If a buyer contacts Nuru about an order, we ask them to contact you first with the payment id.

09Verification and compliance

Before you take live payments, you verify your identity once with a photo of your ID and a quick selfie. It is free. We may ask for more information if details do not match or if the law requires it.

You are responsible for taxes on your sales and income. Privacy on Nuru means a payment is not exposed on a public ledger. It does not mean Nuru will hide a transaction from a lawful request.

10Getting started

  1. Start free with your business email.
  2. Open Business tools and create a Sandbox key.
  3. Create a test payment, by hand or with the API, and save a webhook.
  4. Verify your identity with your ID and a selfie, then create a Live key.
  5. Send buyers to the checkout link and deliver on paid.

11Talk to us

For larger volumes, a marketplace setup, or help with an integration, write to norashillings@benuru.com. For a problem with a live payment, write to supportnorashillings@benuru.com with the payment id.
