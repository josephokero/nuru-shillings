Answered questions

Can I run a store under a name that is not a registered company?

Yes. Give the store a name. Nuru records it on its own servers and shows only that name at checkout. Your personal name is not shown to buyers.

Answered by the Nuru team

My site was refused by a card processor. Will Nuru accept it?

Nuru does not refuse a site because card networks refuse its category, including adult sites. What is illegal where the buyer or the company is located is still not allowed. See Acceptable use.

Answered by the Nuru team

Does a buyer need a Nuru wallet to pay me?

No. A buyer with Nuru Shillings pays from the wallet. A buyer without them pays with M-Pesa, PayPal, or a card on the same checkout.

Answered by the Nuru team

Can anyone trace my payments on a public chain?

No. Payments are kept on Nuru’s private chain and are not written to a public ledger. Checkout shows the company name only.

Answered by the Nuru team

My webhook is not receiving events.

Check that the URL is https and returns a 2xx response quickly. Send a test event from Business tools. Events are payment.paid and payment.refunded.

Answered by the Nuru team

Someone in a group chat offered to “unlock” my withdrawal for a fee.

That is a scam. Nuru never charges to withdraw and never asks for your password, PIN, or secret key. Report it to supportnorashillings@benuru.com.

Answered by the Nuru team

Ask the community

Keep it general. Do not post a payment id, a balance, a password, or a key.

Community rules

  • Be respectful. No harassment, spam, or advertising.
  • Never share passwords, PINs, secret keys, card numbers, or someone else’s details.
  • No help with anything that is not allowed under the Terms.
  • Nuru staff will never message you first asking for money or codes.
