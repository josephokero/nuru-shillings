Developers

Ship checkout from zero to production · Payments API version 1

The short version
  • One request creates a payment: the amount in NSH, your order reference, and the company name the buyer sees.
  • Six routes cover everything: create, read, refund, and three for webhooks.
  • A nsh_test_ key works in Sandbox and a nsh_live_ key works in Live. The requests and responses are the same.
  • A live payment returns a checkout_url. When the buyer pays, you get payment.paid on your webhook.
  • The API and Sandbox are free. You pay 1% of each payment that is paid.

This page is the overview. The full reference, with every field and error, is in the Docs.

01What you can build

The Nora API lets a website, an app, or a backend take payment from any Nora wallet. You keep your own customers, your own orders, and your own product. Nora moves the money, shows the buyer your company name, and tells your server the result.

  • One-off checkout for a shop or a single order;
  • Recurring charges, by creating one payment per period;
  • Marketplaces, where your platform takes the payment and settles with sellers;
  • In-app purchases, credit packs, and paid features;
  • Invoices, using the invoice number as the reference.

02How a payment flows

  1. Your server calls POST /v1/payments with the amount, your reference, and your company name.
  2. Nora returns a payment id. In Live the status is pending and the response includes a checkout_url.
  3. You send the buyer to the checkout_url. They see your company name and the amount, and confirm from their wallet.
  4. The status becomes paid. The amount less 1% is added to your balance, and Nora sends payment.paid to your webhook.
  5. Your server confirms the payment with GET /v1/payments/:id and delivers the order.

03Quick start

This takes about two minutes in Sandbox.

  1. Start free and open the sign-in link. Your account starts with 10,000 Sandbox NSH.
  2. Open Business tools, keep the switch on Sandbox, and choose Create key. Copy it; it is shown once.
  3. Store it on your server as NURU_SECRET_KEY, then create a payment:
curl https://norashillings.benuru.com/v1/payments \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $NURU_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "amount": "1800",
    "unit": "NSH",
    "reference": "order_1042",
    "company": "Northwind Books"
  }'

In Sandbox the payment is paid at once and 1,782 Sandbox NSH are added to your balance. The same call from Node.js:

const res = await fetch("https://norashillings.benuru.com/v1/payments", {
  method: "POST",
  headers: {
    Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.NURU_SECRET_KEY}`,
    "Content-Type": "application/json",
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({ amount: "1800", unit: "NSH", reference: "order_1042", company: "Northwind Books" }),
});
const payment = await res.json();

04Endpoints

Base URL: https://norashillings.benuru.com/v1. Send JSON over https. Every response is JSON.

Method and pathWhat it does
POST /v1/paymentsCreate a payment
GET /v1/payments/:idRetrieve one payment
POST /v1/refundsRefund a paid payment
POST /v1/webhooksSave or clear the webhook URL for this mode
GET /v1/webhooksRead the saved webhook URL
POST /v1/webhooks/testSend a test event to the saved URL

05The payment object

{
  "id": "pay_8f2c41d09a7be3150c66",
  "status": "pending",
  "amount": "1800",
  "unit": "NSH",
  "fee": "18",
  "company": "Northwind Books",
  "chain": "private",
  "reference": "order_1042",
  "mode": "live",
  "created": "2026-10-10T09:30:00.000Z",
  "checkout_url": "https://norashillings.benuru.com/pay?id=pay_8f2c41d09a7be3150c66"
}
StatusMeaning
pendingCreated in Live and waiting for the buyer. No money has moved.
paidThe buyer was charged and you were credited, less the fee.
refundedThe full amount went back to the buyer and the fee came back to you.

Amounts are whole NSH sent as strings, from "1" to "100000000", so nothing is lost to rounding. Treat any status other than paid as not paid.

07Webhooks

Save an https URL with POST /v1/webhooks or in Business tools. Nora sends a POST with a JSON body when a payment changes.

EventSent when
payment.paidA payment becomes paid
payment.refundedA payment is refunded
webhook.testYou send a test event

Handle an event safely

  1. Reply with a 2xx at once, then do the slow work.
  2. Read data.id and call GET /v1/payments/:id with your key. Trust that response, not the event body.
  3. Check the status is paid and the reference and amount match your order.
  4. Record the event id, and ignore it if it arrives again.

Every event and the status your server returned are listed under Webhook events in Business tools.

08Refunds

POST /v1/refunds

{ "payment": "pay_8f2c41d09a7be3150c66" }

A refund returns the full amount to the buyer’s wallet and returns the 1% fee to you, so it costs nothing extra. Only a paid payment can be refunded, and only once. You can also refund from the payments list in Business tools.

09Keys and security

  • Shown once. Nora keeps only a one-way hash of a key and its last four characters.
  • Server only. Never place a key in a web page, a mobile app, a public repository, or a log.
  • Replace at once. Replace key stops the old key in that mode immediately.
  • Live needs verification. A live key is only created for a verified account.

10Errors

A failed request returns a status and one field, error, that says what to fix.

StatusMeaning
400The request cannot be done as sent, for example an invalid amount
401No valid key
403Live key used before the account is verified
404The payment does not exist, or belongs to another account or mode
500A problem on Nora’s side. Try again

11Going live

  1. Your integration works end to end in Sandbox, including a refund and a webhook.
  2. Your account is verified, and you created a live key with the switch on Live.
  3. The live key is stored on your server only.
  4. Your live webhook URL passes Send test event.
  5. You deliver only after a confirmed paid, and duplicate events cannot deliver twice.

12Pricing

The API, Sandbox, keys, and webhooks are free. You pay 1% of each payment that reaches paid. A payment that is never paid costs nothing, and a refund returns the fee. See Business pricing for examples.
