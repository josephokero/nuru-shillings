Product

One wallet to add, hold, send, pay, and withdraw · Updated 10 October 2026

The short version
  • Nora Shillings (NSH) is one balance you can spend on any platform that connects the Nora payments API.
  • The rate never moves: 100 NSH = 1 US dollar, to buy, to pay, to refund, and to sell.
  • Add money with PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa. Each method opens its own checkout, and you get a receipt on screen and by email.
  • Send to another Nora account by email, pay a company at checkout, and withdraw to PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa.
  • The account, the wallet, adding money, sending, and withdrawing are free. There is no monthly fee.

You can add, send, pay, and withdraw as soon as you sign up. Identity verification is not required.

01What Nora Shillings is

Nora Shillings is a wallet and a payment network. You buy Nora Shillings once, hold them in your wallet, and spend them on any shop, app, marketplace, or site that accepts Nora. The same balance works everywhere it is accepted, in every country.

When you pay a company, the checkout shows the company name and the amount. The payment is settled on Nora’s private chain. It is not written to a public ledger where anyone can follow the amount, the time, and the path.

Nora is payment infrastructure. It is not a bank, an investment, or a savings product. A balance does not earn interest, and its value is fixed at the published rate.

02The wallet

Every account opens with a wallet. The overview shows:

  • Your balance in NSH, with the dollar value beside it;
  • Available and pending amounts, so a withdrawal under review is never counted twice;
  • Money in and money out for the period, and the number of movements;
  • Recent activity, with a link to the full list;
  • A converter that turns dollars into NSH and back, at 100 NSH to 1 USD.

Each part of the wallet has its own page in the sidebar: Overview, Activity, Add money, Send money, Withdraw, and Account. On a phone the sidebar folds into a menu.

03Adding money

Any wallet can add money with three methods, straight after sign-up. They are separate: you choose one, and that method’s own checkout opens.

Amounts

  • You choose a whole amount from 1 to 10,000 US dollars. Every dollar becomes 100 NSH.
  • For M-Pesa, the checkout shows the amount in Kenyan shillings before you confirm. A single M-Pesa payment can be up to KES 250,000.
  • The summary beside the form shows the amount, the method, what lands in your wallet, and your balance afterwards.

How M-Pesa works

  1. Prompt sent. You enter your number and choose Send M-Pesa prompt. Nora asks M-Pesa to send a payment request to that phone.
  2. Enter your PIN. A pop-up appears on your phone with the amount. You enter your M-Pesa PIN. The wallet shows a countdown and waits for M-Pesa.
  3. Confirmed or declined. If M-Pesa confirms, the wallet is credited and the receipt appears. If you cancel, the PIN is wrong, the balance is too low, or the prompt times out, the wallet tells you why and nothing is charged.

Receipts

Every successful payment shows a receipt at once and sends the same receipt to your email. It lists the receipt number, the date, the method you paid with, the amount paid, the M-Pesa or PayPal transaction number, the NSH added, and your new balance. A payment is credited once, even if the confirmation arrives more than once.

04Sending money

You can send Nora Shillings to anyone with a Nora account by entering the email they signed up with. The money moves at once and appears in both activity lists. People you sent to before are shown as shortcuts.

  • Sending is free. The person receives the full amount.
  • You cannot send more than your available balance, and you cannot send to yourself.
  • If no account uses that email, nothing is sent. Ask them to sign up first.

05Paying a company

When a platform accepts Nora, it sends you to a Nora checkout that shows the company name and the amount. You confirm from your wallet. If your balance covers it, the payment is paid and the company is told at once. If it does not, nothing moves.

The buyer pays exactly the amount shown. Nora adds nothing on top; the company pays the 1% fee. If the company refunds, the full amount returns to your wallet at the same rate.

06Selling and withdrawing

You can sell your balance back at 100 NSH to 1 USD and withdraw it to PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa in your verified name. Withdrawing is free.

The amount leaves your available balance as soon as you submit, and the withdrawal shows as pending in your activity until it is sent. If the method rejects it, the amount returns to your wallet.

07Activity and records

The Activity page lists every movement in a table: what it was, the date, the method, the status, and the amount. You can:

  • filter by money in, money out, or pending;
  • search by name, email, method, or amount;
  • download the list as a CSV file for your own records.

08Your account

Sign-in

There is no password. You enter your email, and we send a sign-in link that works once. On that device you stay logged in, even after closing the browser, until you log out.

Profile

You can add a profile picture. It is stored privately with your account and shown in your wallet. Your choices, such as your last payment method and your M-Pesa number, are remembered so checkout is faster next time.

Verification

Identity verification is optional. You can add money, send, pay, and withdraw without it. Didit ID checks are not required to use the wallet or Business tools.

11Privacy and safety

  • Card numbers and PayPal passwords are entered on PayPal’s page. Nora never sees them.
  • Your M-Pesa PIN is entered on your own phone. Nora never asks for it.
  • A payment is only credited after PayPal or M-Pesa confirms it from their own records.
  • Checkout shows the company, not the person paying.

Read more on the Security page and in the Privacy Notice.

12Getting started

  1. Start free with your email and open the sign-in link.
  2. Add money with PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa.
  3. Send to a friend or pay a company that accepts Nora.
  4. When you want to withdraw, verify with your ID and a selfie on the Account page.
