04The service

Nora is payment infrastructure. A person can buy Nora Shillings, hold them, and spend them on any platform that connects the API. A company can take that payment inside its own product. Checkout shows the company name only. The payment record is marked chain: "private" and is not written to a public ledger.

Nora is not a bank, an investment product, or a savings account. A balance does not earn interest and is not covered by a deposit-insurance scheme. Nora Shillings are not a share, a security, or a speculative asset; their value is fixed at the rate in section 07.

Nora moves value between a wallet and a company and returns the result to the platform. Nora is not the seller of what a company sells, and it is not a party to the sale between the buyer and the company.

12Refunds, refusals, and errors

If a payment was recorded in error, for example a duplicate created by a platform fault, we may correct it by reversing it and will show the correction in the activity list. If Nora credits your wallet by mistake, you must not spend that amount and we may remove it.
