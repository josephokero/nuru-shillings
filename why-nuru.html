Why Nuru

What is different, and what is not · Updated 10 October 2026

The short version
  • Checkout shows the company. The person paying is not put on display, and nothing is written to a public ledger.
  • One rate everywhere: 100 NSH = 1 USD. No spread, no second rate, no surprise at checkout.
  • Money added to a wallet is available as soon as the payment method confirms it.
  • Buyers can pay from a Nuru balance, or add money with PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa.
  • Lawful businesses that card processors refuse, including adult sites, can take payment.

Nuru is payment infrastructure, not a bank or an investment. Section 08 explains what that means.

02Privacy by default

Most payment trails can be followed. A card charge carries the buyer’s name to the seller and to every network in between. A public blockchain lets anyone trace the amount, the time, and the path forever.

Nuru works differently. The checkout shows the company that is being paid. The payment is settled on Nuru’s private chain and is marked chain: "private". Only the buyer, the company, and Nuru can match a payment id to a payment.

Card numbers and PayPal passwords are entered on PayPal’s own page, and M-Pesa PINs on the buyer’s own phone. Nuru never sees them.

03One fixed rate

A Nuru Shilling is always one cent. Ten dollars is 1,000 NSH. Eighteen dollars is 1,800 NSH. The same rate is used to buy, to pay, to calculate the fee, to refund, and to sell back.

A shop in one country and a buyer in another see the same number, so the amount on the page is the amount that moves. Nuru does not quote a second rate and does not add a spread.

04Money that lands at once

When you add money, the wallet waits only for PayPal or M-Pesa to confirm, then credits it. With M-Pesa that is usually seconds after you enter your PIN. You get a receipt on screen and by email.

A payment to a company moves the balance the moment the buyer confirms. The company sees paid at once and receives the webhook.

05Every buyer can pay

A buyer is not forced onto one rail. If they hold Nuru Shillings, they spend that balance. If they do not, they add money with M-Pesa, PayPal, or a card and pay. Each method opens its own checkout: PayPal takes them to PayPal, a card takes them to a secure card page, and M-Pesa sends a prompt to their phone.

The business keeps one integration instead of stitching separate processors together.

06Open to more businesses

Card processors decline whole categories, including adult content, even when the business is lawful. Nuru does not refuse a lawful business for its category. Shops, apps, marketplaces, creators, and adult sites can all take payment.

That openness has limits. Fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion, and anything illegal where the business or buyer is located are not accepted. See Acceptable use.

07Simple to build, simple to price

  • One request creates a payment. Six routes cover the whole API.
  • Sandbox is free, works at once, and needs no verification.
  • The company pays 1% of a paid payment. Refusals are free, refunds return the fee, and there is no monthly fee.
  • The buyer pays exactly the amount shown.

08What Nuru is not

Being clear about this matters as much as the features:

  • Not a bank. A balance is not a bank deposit and is not covered by deposit insurance.
  • Not an investment. Nuru Shillings do not earn interest and do not go up or down. 100 NSH is always 1 USD.
  • Not a public cryptocurrency. There is no token to trade and no public ledger.
  • Not a way to break the law. Privacy means a payment is not exposed in public. Nuru still meets lawful requests from authorities that have power over it.

09How we keep it safe

  • Sign-in uses one-time email links. There is no password to steal or reuse.
  • Money only leaves Nuru after you verify your identity with your ID and a selfie, and only to accounts in that name.
  • A top-up is only credited after PayPal or M-Pesa confirms it from their own records, and only once.
  • API keys are stored as one-way hashes and can be replaced at once.

The details are on the Security page.

10Questions

Is Nuru only for Kenya?

No. Buyers and businesses can be in any country. M-Pesa is one of several ways to add money; PayPal and cards work worldwide.

What does it cost me as a buyer?

Nothing from Nuru. Adding money, sending, paying, and withdrawing are free. PayPal, your card issuer, or M-Pesa may charge their own fees.

Can I get my money out?

Yes. Sell your balance back at 100 NSH to 1 USD and withdraw to PayPal, a card, or M-Pesa in your name, for free.

Can I try it first?

Yes. Every account starts with 10,000 practice NSH in Sandbox. Nothing there is real money.
